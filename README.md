# ZeroFootPrint
ZeroFootPrint is a smart campus app dedicated to driving Carbon Footprint Reduction within the university community. Instead of buying new academic materials and electronics that heavily rely on carbon intensive manufacturing, we swap items for free or at symbolic prices. 

The Problem: 
University campuses generate massive amounts of carbon emissions and waste due to the continuous, repetitive purchasing of new academic materials such as textbooks, engineering tools, lab coats, and electronics every semester. Manufacturing and shipping these items heavily accelerate global warming.

The Target User: 
University students suffer from high financial expenses when buying new equipment, while other students have the exact same items sitting completely unused in their rooms.

Evidence and Statistics:
Studies show that producing a single new textbook emits around 7.5 kg of CO2, and electronic devices generate even more. By swapping 3 items per student, a single campus can prevent tons of greenhouse gas emissions annually, directly contributing to a circular economy.
